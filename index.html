About

Oisin Ocean is a music project by musician and visual artist Alex Sharkov. All aspects of the project are created and handled by him. The project began in 2023 and is currently based in Europe. On this website, you can find Oisin Ocean's music, photographs, unreleased material, and more. Thank you for visiting the site.
